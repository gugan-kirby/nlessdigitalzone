Why doctors choose us

We Know Where the Line Is

Your patients already searched for you — the question is what they found. The NMC treats a post that solicits patients as advertising. That doesn't mean you can't market; it means you need someone who has read the rules.

Invisible where it counts

Patients search “best dentist near me”, not your name. A stale Google Business Profile keeps you out of that list.

Marketing feels risky

One badly worded caption or a patient photo posted without consent can become a real problem.

Agencies don't speak medicine

“Guaranteed results” sells shoes. For a doctor it is a compliance problem waiting to happen.

What we build for you

  • Educational content that explains conditions, prevention and procedures in plain language
  • Factual listings — your qualifications, registration, services, timings and location
  • A properly maintained Google Business Profile and clean directory presence
  • Genuine patient reviews, requested the right way and answered professionally
  • Written consent collected and filed before any patient image or story is used

What we will never post

  • Guaranteed cures, promised outcomes or “100% success” claims of any kind
  • Comparisons that rank you above named doctors or competing hospitals
  • Patient photographs, scans or reports without documented written consent
  • Testimonials engineered to imply a specific clinical outcome
  • Public treatment advice or prescribing in comments and DMs

Plain English: we are a marketing team, not your legal advisor. We write to published NMC guidance and send everything for your approval before it goes out — the final call on any clinical claim is always yours.

About us

Marketing for Doctors. Only.

N'LESS Digital Zone is a small digital marketing team that works only with doctors and clinics. Not restaurants, not real estate, not e-commerce. One industry, learned properly.

A generalist agency will happily write “guaranteed results” or “the best clinic in the city” for you. In medicine that is a compliance problem — the NMC prohibits guaranteed cures, comparative claims against other doctors, and patient imagery used without documented consent.

That focus is also why our pricing starts at ₹7,000 rather than ₹40,000 — we are built for the solo practitioner and the single clinic, the people most agencies ignore.

Nothing. It's a genuine free review — we look at your Google Business Profile, social accounts and website, and send you a written list of what's missing. There's no obligation to buy anything afterwards.

Your clinic name and city is enough for the audit. If you decide to work with us, we'll then need your qualifications and registration details, clinic timings, services list, and any photographs you already have.
